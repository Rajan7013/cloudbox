About CloudBox

Why CloudBox?

A native Android workspace that puts your Google Drive front and center — with zero middlemen.

💡

Core Idea

Turn Google Drive into a fast, clutter-free native Android workspace where every file stays inside the user's own Google Drive account — using least-privilege access (drive.file) with zero intermediary developer servers.

🎯

Aim

Give Android users a lightweight, responsive, privacy-first cloud file manager with built-in multi-format viewers and document scanning.

🚀

Goal

Simplify everyday cloud workflows — uploading, scanning paperwork to PDF, organizing nested folders, collaborating with permissions, and reading offline — across phones, foldables, and tablets.

🔭

Vision

Personal cloud management where users retain 100% ownership of their files, storage quota, and privacy while enjoying effortless native speed.

🛡️

Mission

Deliver a transparent, zero-ad, zero-tracking Google Drive client that requests only the minimum permissions needed and never stores user files on third-party servers.

How It Works

Your Data, Your Drive, Your Rules

How does CloudBox work?

Each user signs in with their own Google account. All uploads, folders, and files live strictly in their own Google Drive and use their own Google storage quota.

CloudBox communicates directly with Google Drive APIs on your device. There is no developer server in between — your files never pass through anyone else's infrastructure.

🔒 Neither the app developer nor other users can ever access your files. Your data stays 100% yours.
